Privacy policy

Lumin ERP is a mobile app for customers of the Lumen business suite. It shows the Lumen web app to business users who already have an account.

What we collect. Your name and email address to sign you in, and the business records you or your colleagues enter in Lumen, such as jobs, customers, stock, invoices and staff details.

What we do not do. No advertising. No tracking across other apps or websites. We never sell your data or share it for marketing.

How it is used. Only to run the service for your business. Your records belong to your business and are visible only to the people your business has given access to.

Camera and photos. The app asks for these only if you choose to attach a photo to a record.

Deleting your data. Email prathap.bb@gmail.com and ask. We will delete your account and personal data, and confirm by reply.

Support

Need help, or found a problem? Email prathap.bb@gmail.com. Tell us your business name and what you saw on screen. Lumin ERP needs an internet connection and an existing Lumen account.
